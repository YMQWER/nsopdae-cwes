Twenty Thousand Leagues

Under the Sea

A Classic Science Fiction Novel by Jules Verne

"The sea is everything. It covers seven tenths of the terrestrial globe. Its breath is pure and healthy. It is an immense desert, where man is never lonely, for he feels life stirring on all sides."

The Synopsis

Published in 1870, this masterpiece follows Professor Pierre Aronnax, his servant Conseil, and the harpooner Ned Land. After being cast overboard during an expedition to find a mysterious "sea monster," they are rescued—or rather, captured—by the enigmatic Captain Nemo aboard the Nautilus, a technologically advanced submarine.

The Crew & Guests

Captain Nemo

A brilliant scientist and a brooding exile who has severed all ties with humanity. He explores the ocean depths to find a freedom the land cannot provide.

Professor Aronnax

A French marine biologist whose intellectual curiosity drives the narrative, documenting the wonders of the deep through a scientific lens.

Ned Land

The "King of Harpooners." Unlike Aronnax, he feels imprisoned by the Nautilus and spends the journey plotting their escape back to civilization.

Key Milestones

  • The Underwater Forest: Hunting in the submarine forests of Crespo Island using pneumatic rifles.
  • The Lost Continent: Walking through the ruins of the legendary Atlantis.
  • The Battle with the Kraken: A terrifying encounter with giant squids that claimed the life of a crew member.
  • The South Pole: Risking everything to plant a flag at the pole, nearly suffocating beneath the ice.
